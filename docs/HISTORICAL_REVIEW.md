# Historical-accuracy review — regions 1 & 2 (Phase 6)

A review pass over the authored content for Lower Egypt and the
Sahara/Sahel corridor, per DESIGN.md §3.2 and roadmap step 6. Findings and
fixes below; the design intent (facts stated, never dramatized; the player is
a private individual dependent on hospitality) is unchanged.

## Findings — correct as authored

- **Polities**: Alexandria/Cairo/Asyut/Luxor/Aswan under the Khedivate of
  Egypt (British-occupied since 1882 — correct for the 1890s); Kufra under
  the Sanusi brotherhood (held since 1895); Zinder under the Sultanate of
  Damagaram (French conquest 1899, after our setting); Kano under the Sokoto
  Caliphate (British conquest 1903, after our setting).
- **Geography**: the Nile's two delta branches (Rosetta/Damietta), Cairo at
  the apex, Aswan at the first cataract, the Darb el-Arbain route toward the
  Kharga and Dakhla oases, Kufra in the eastern desert, Lake Chad, Kano in
  the Sahel. The map is schematic but directionally consistent.
- **Goods & currency**: coffee, trade beads, gold dust, salt, cloth, dates —
  all trans-Saharan staples; piastres was the Ottoman-Egyptian currency.
- **Medicine**: quinine for malaria, boiled water for dysentery, night
  travel against sunstroke, avoidance of tsetse country for trypanosomiasis
  — all period-correct countermeasures.
- **Names**: Arabic (Hakim Ahmed, Sheikh Yusuf, Elder Bashir, Sheikhah
  Fatima), Coptic (Priest Ankhmose), Hausa (Malam Idris, Hajja Amina) —
  plausible for their settlements.

## Fixes applied

| Item | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kharga & Dakhla polity | "Ottoman Egypt (southern frontier)" | "Khedivate of Egypt" | The oases were administered within the Khedivate; a separate "Ottoman frontier" label overstated their status |

## Non-goals of this pass

Regions 3–5 (Congo, Great Lakes/Swahili coast, Southern Africa) remain
post-v1; their research pass happens with their content (DESIGN.md §3.2).
